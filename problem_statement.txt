24 to 48-Hour Hackathon Challenge
Executive Summary
Design and build an intelligent, AI-powered platform that transforms hackathon management by
automating the entire lifecycle—from participant registration to winner announcement—through
a unified dashboard. The solution should leverage machine learning, natural language
processing, and predictive analytics to address critical challenges around fairness, transparency,
and scalability across all types of hackathons (corporate, open, college, community).
Expected Outcome: A working prototype that demonstrates how AI can solve real hackathon
management problems while reducing organizer workload and improving participant experience
within 24 to 48 hours.
